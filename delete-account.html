Delete your PlayNook account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open PlayNook and tap the Profile button (top right of the home screen).
  2. Tap Delete account, then Delete forever.

Deletion is immediate.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email vanlinh.58th1@gmail.com with the subject "PlayNook account deletion", from the Google account you signed in with (so we can confirm it is yours). We delete it within 7 days and reply when done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and the email linked to it
  • Your display name, tag and profile photo link
  • All your scores, ranks and play counts
  • Your score submission log

Nothing is kept on our servers afterwards. Best scores saved on your own device stay there until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
