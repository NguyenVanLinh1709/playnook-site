PlayNook

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 8, 2026 · Applies to the Android app "PlayNook: Mini Games" (package learning_battle.com.vn), version 2.0.0 and later.

PlayNook is made by an independent developer ("we"). This page explains what the app collects, why, and how you can delete it. In short: you can play without an account, and then nothing about you leaves your phone. If you sign in, we store only what the leaderboards need.

1. Playing as a guest

Without signing in, the app keeps your settings (sound, vibration, screen shake) and your best scores only on your device. Nothing about you is sent to our servers; only an anonymous crash report is sent if the app crashes (see section 4).

2. Signing in with Google (optional)

If you choose "Sign in with Google", Google shares with us your email address, name and profile photo. We use them as follows:

  • Email: identifies your account. It is never shown to other players.
  • Google name: only used to suggest a display name the first time; you choose the name that is shown.
  • Profile photo: shown next to your name on leaderboards, unless you turn on "Hide profile photo" (then a generated pixel avatar is shown instead).

3. What we store when you are signed in

  • Your display name and a random 4-digit tag (public on leaderboards).
  • For each game: your best score, the duration of that round, the date it was achieved and how many rounds you played (the best score and rank are public).
  • A log of score submissions (score, round duration, app version, time, and whether it was accepted). It is used to stop cheating and spam, and is deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • The first time an account signs in on a device, that device's best scores are added to the account.

The app also keeps your sign-in session on the device in encrypted storage, and may keep finished rounds there until it can send them (for example while you are offline).

4. Who processes the data

  • Supabase (supabase.com) hosts our database and sign-in service.
  • Google provides "Sign in with Google".
  • Sentry (sentry.io) receives crash reports, signed in or not. A report contains the error and where in the app it happened, the steps just before it (such as which screen was open and which button was tapped), the device model, Android version and app version. It does not contain your name, email, account ID, scores or screenshots, and we do not store your IP address. We use reports only to find and fix bugs; Sentry deletes them after 90 days at most.

We do not sell your data, do not share it for advertising, and the app contains no ads and no analytics. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

5. Deleting your data

Open the app → Profile → Delete account. Your account, display name, scores and submission log are deleted from our servers immediately. Best scores saved on your device stay on your device. If you cannot use the app, see Delete your account to ask by email.

Signing out (without deleting) removes the session and any unsent scores from the device.

6. Children

PlayNook is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Earlier versions (Dual Minds)

Versions before 2.0.0 of this app were a different game ("Dual Minds") on a separate server. That data is not carried over to PlayNook and the old server will be deleted.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
