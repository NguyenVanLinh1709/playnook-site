PlayNook

Mini games, one nook

PlayNook is a small collection of pixel-art mini games for Android. Open it, pick a game and play straight away: no account needed.

Jumping Ninja

Tap to jump. Slip through the gaps between the stone pillars at sunset, one point for each. It gets faster as you go.

Rune Blocks

Drag the rune stones onto the board. Fill a row or a column to clear it, and keep clearing turn after turn for a bigger combo.

Lantern Link

Link each pair of lanterns with a glowing thread. Threads can't cross, and every tile must be filled. Solve as many as you can before time runs out.

Spirit Vials

Pour glowing spirit essences between vials until each vial holds a single color. Every pour costs a move, and each solved puzzle earns more.

Sky Pagoda

Tap to drop each floor onto the pagoda. Whatever hangs over the edge is cut off, so land it just right. Climb from the sunset into the night sky.

Leaderboards

Sign in with Google if you want your name on the global leaderboards. You pick a display name (shown with a 4-digit tag like Linh #4821); your email is never shown to other players. You can hide your Google photo or delete your account at any time from the Profile screen.
